# Privacy policy — Job Match

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Job Match is a skill: a set of instructions that Claude follows inside your own Claude session. It has
no server, no database, no analytics and no account. The author receives none of your data.

## What the skill reads

- **Your CV**, from the file you attach (Claude app) or from the folder where you started the session
  (Claude Code), or your LinkedIn profile if you export it as PDF or paste its text yourself.
- **Public job postings**, through Claude's web search and page reading tools: company career pages,
  the public job feeds of recruiting platforms, and search results.

Your CV is used only to compare your profile with job postings. It is not sent to job sites, and the
search queries hold only things like the role, technologies and location.

## What the skill writes

Only when you ask for it, a tailored CV and a cover letter. They are saved in your own folder
(Claude Code) or given to you as a download (Claude app). Nothing is written anywhere else.

## Where your data goes

Your CV and the conversation are processed by Claude under your agreement with Anthropic and
[Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). The skill adds no other
recipient.

## Retention

The skill keeps nothing. What stays in your conversation history, and for how long, is set by your
Claude account settings.

## Age

The skill is not intended for people under 18.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/caio-t/job-match/issues>.
